Academy privacy notice

Updated 26 September 2026. This notice explains the additional information used for the ChessVerseAI Academy workspace.

Information we process

We process account and organization details, membership roles, student and coach records, batches, assignments, recorded training, shared games and progress reports. Billing records include your legal name, email, address, country, optional GSTIN, selected plan and payment references.

Why we use it

This information supports account verification, academy administration, access controls, learning reports, payment verification, invoices, troubleshooting and security.

Who can access it

Access is controlled by organization membership and permissions. Academy administrators and authorized coaches can access the records their roles permit. Operational service providers process information as needed for hosting, email and payments.

Payments

Payment details are entered through Razorpay checkout. Our academy billing system stores order and payment references and invoice details; it does not store card CVVs or UPI PINs. Razorpay processes payment information under its privacy policy.

Students and children

Academies must ensure they have appropriate authority and parent or guardian involvement before adding children’s records. Share only information needed for chess education.

Retention and your choices

Contact us to request access, correction or deletion. We verify requests and may need to retain billing or security records for legal obligations. Subscription expiry does not automatically delete your records. See the account deletion information.

Related notice and contact

The ChessVerseAI Privacy Policy covers the underlying account and consumer app. Questions about academy information: contactus@epitomehub.com.
