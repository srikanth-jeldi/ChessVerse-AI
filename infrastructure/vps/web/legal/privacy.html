ChessVerseAI Privacy Policy

Effective: July 27, 2026

ChessVerseAI, operated by EpitomeHub, provides chess play, challenges, coaching and account features.

Information we process

We process account identifiers such as display name and email, authentication records, game history, settings, and technical security logs. Google Sign-In provides an account identifier, name and email when you choose that login method. We do not receive your Google password.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, save games and progress, provide chess features, prevent abuse, maintain security, and support users. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and sharing

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored by our hosting and service providers only as needed to operate ChessVerseAI. We may disclose information when legally required or to protect users and the service.

Retention and deletion

We retain account data while the account is active and as required for security or legal obligations. You may request account and associated personal-data deletion using our data deletion page.

Children

ChessVerseAI is not directed to children under the minimum age required to consent to online services in their country without parent or guardian involvement.
