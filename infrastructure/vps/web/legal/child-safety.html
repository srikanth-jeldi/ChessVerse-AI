Child Safety Standards

Effective: August 21, 2026

ChessVerseAI prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, grooming, sexualization of minors, trafficking, sextortion and any content or conduct that endangers a child.

Prevention and enforcement

Users must meet the applicable minimum age or participate with parent or guardian involvement. We may remove content, restrict accounts, preserve relevant evidence and report apparent child sexual abuse material or credible threats to appropriate authorities where required.

Reporting

Report child-safety concerns immediately to chessverseai@gmail.com. Include the relevant username and enough information to locate the content; do not forward or reproduce illegal imagery. If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
