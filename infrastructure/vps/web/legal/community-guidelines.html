Community Guidelines

Effective: August 21, 2026

ChessVerseAI is a place to play, learn and connect respectfully.

Be respectful

No harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, bullying, impersonation, doxxing, scams or repeated unwanted contact.

Keep content safe and lawful

Share only content you have the right to share. Do not upload malware, illegal material, private credentials or content that exploits or endangers a child.

Play fairly

No engines, bots, collusion, rating manipulation, account sharing or intentional connection abuse in competitive play.

Enforcement and reports

Violations may result in content removal, feature restrictions, rating correction, suspension or termination. Report a username, message, attachment or game with relevant details to chessverseai@gmail.com.
