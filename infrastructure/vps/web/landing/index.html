What is ChessVerseAI?

ChessVerseAI is a chess platform by EpitomeHub for live play, computer games, AI-assisted review, puzzle training, tournaments and chess community features.

Can I play without installing the app?

Yes. Launch the full web experience from this site, or install the Android app for a mobile-first experience.

Is ChessVerseAI suitable for beginners?

Yes. Multiple puzzle difficulty paths, guided practice and clear game review help newer players learn while advanced players can focus on competitive play.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Use friend challenges, private rooms or local over-the-board mode. You can also connect through friends, clubs and private chat.

Does my progress sync?

Account-based cloud progression is designed to keep your identity and progress available across supported devices.

Where can I manage privacy or delete my account?

Use the Privacy and Data deletion links in the footer. ChessVerseAI also publishes Terms, Community Guidelines and Child Safety Standards.
