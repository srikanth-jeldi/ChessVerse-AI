- Club streak header: constrain long day-count text.
- Social hub: flexible section headings and wrapped player actions.
- Tournament: content-driven hero height and wrapping badges, points and
  pairing labels, without invalid Spacer use inside Wrap.
- Authentication: flexible forgot-password and divider labels.
- Game import: scrollable format chooser on narrow screens.
- Sprint leaderboard: clear loading state for guest users.
- Saved analysis: flexible metadata labels.
- Academy lesson: flexible language action for long translations.
- Missions: wrap progress and claim controls.
- Academy selector: expand within available width, variable-height options.
