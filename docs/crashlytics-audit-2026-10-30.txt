SOURCE FIX
- Retain a single native initialization across bounded caller waits.
- Publish late readiness so mounted banner slots can load automatically.
- Retry empty banner slots on foreground/network recovery; avoid duplicate loads.
- Avoid starting ad work in background or when network health is known offline.
- Eight-second caller deferral becomes a breadcrumb, not a false SDK-failure event.
- Retain actual native errors (including late errors) and report a genuinely
  stalled operation once after 35 seconds. No blanket Crashlytics filtering.
- No new per-device ad suppression or SDK removal.
