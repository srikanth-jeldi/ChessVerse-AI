Home text layout fix (27 October 2026)

Long account names now wrap without ellipsis. The header separates identity
and controls from the wrapping coin actions in both phone and wide layouts.

scale; titles and descriptions no longer have fixed line limits. AI rival
cards retain two columns on phones and wrap the icon/rating header when needed.
Shortcut rows reserve enough vertical space for enlarged labels. Dashboard
panels and ranking rows grow with their content rather than fixed heights.
