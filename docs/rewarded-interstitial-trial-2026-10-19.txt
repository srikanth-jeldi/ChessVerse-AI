Rewarded interstitial trial v1

Source default OFF. Release 1.3.38+305 enables the 10% trial via mobile/config/release-305.json.
Signed artifact preparation is authorized; Play Store publishing has not been performed.
Enable a release only with both:
  --dart-define=REWARDED_INTERSTITIAL_TRIAL_ENABLED=true
  --dart-define=ADMOB_ANDROID_REWARDED_INTERSTITIAL_ID=<dedicated real rewarded-interstitial ad unit>
Do not reuse the normal rewarded or interstitial IDs. Configure the unit reward
as 24 hours of advanced Mistake Bank hints. Android only. Debug uses Google test ID.

Random, persisted per-installation bucket: 10% treatment, 90% control.
Computer/online completed matches only, at existing post-match navigation.
Treatment replaces normal interstitial; no fallback after skip, no-fill or error.
Shared display cap/cooldown: at most 2 treatment displays/day, 5 minutes apart.
Shared lease also excludes existing coin/hint rewarded displays while intro/ad open.
Intro explicitly describes reward and includes Skip. Reward only on SDK reward callback.
Reward is device-local, saved for 24 hours, consumed by advanced Mistake Bank hints.
Storage failure preserves current-session entitlement and records diagnostics.

Firebase event post_match_ad_trial: assigned/displayed/intro_skipped/load_failed/
show_failed/reward_earned, with arm. User property post_match_ad_trial_arm.
Compare revenue/active user and retention by arm over a complete observation window;
do not conclude success from eCPM alone. Experiment also changes the treatment cap.
Disable with REWARDED_INTERSTITIAL_TRIAL_ENABLED=false in the next build; this is
a compile-time kill switch, not remote config. Already earned rewards remain valid.
