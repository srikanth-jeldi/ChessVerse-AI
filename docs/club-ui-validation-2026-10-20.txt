Club features and language layout validation — 20 October 2026

Implemented
- Club-specific Challenges tab, sent/received labels and expiration states.
- Sending a challenge selects the Challenges tab.
- Challenge API checks membership and filters matches by club.
- Reusing a pending challenge belonging to another club is rejected.
- Create your club form; creator receives OWNER membership transactionally.
- Up to three currently owned clubs per player, unique name validation.
- Exact registered email/@username invitations through app notifications.
  Invitations do not send email or automatically join the recipient.
- Invitation membership checks, daily deduplication and 20/day sender limit.
- New club labels translated into all 34 supported languages.
- Fixed post-game action wrapping: three equal-width actions with wrapping
  inside each button. Removed stale English language-selection snackbars.
- Fixed detected narrow-screen overflows in auth, home, options, analysis,
  daily challenge, master games, Learn Chess and leaderboard layouts.
